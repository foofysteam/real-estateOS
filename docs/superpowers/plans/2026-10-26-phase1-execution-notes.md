## Known gaps carried into later plans

- Stuck `PROCESSING` webhook rows (crash mid-processing) cannot be retried from the UI; needs a `claimedAt` column and a sweep.
- Sessions are stateless: password change, reset or deactivation does not revoke cookies already issued (30-day expiry). Consider a `sessionVersion` on `User`.
- No rate limiting on login, the login action, `/api/ingest` or `changePasswordAction`.
- Cookie-authenticated route handlers (`/api/ingest/csv`, `/api/auth/logout`) rely on `SameSite=Lax` only; add an `Origin`/`Sec-Fetch-Site` check.
- Meta webhook and Graph payloads are hand-validated (tested) rather than Zod schemas.
- Default Meta MCP tool names are unverified against Meta's live server; `META_MCP_TOOL_MAP` overrides them and the Ads screen lists the real names when one is missing.
- No manual "New lead" screen for walk-ins/referrals (spec gap); the Pipeline empty-state CTA points at `/leads`.
- Sidebar is not collapsible (spec §3 says collapsible).
- Keyboard drag is the only way to move a pipeline card on desktop (Move button hidden at `lg`), with no screen-reader announcements.
- `src/lib/db.ts` validates env at import, so `next build` needs the env present.
